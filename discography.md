# Every Kanye West song, verse, and feature: 2003–May 2026

A chronological catalog assembled from Genius, Discogs, Wikipedia, AllMusic, the Kanye West Fandom Wiki, KanyeToThe/KTT2, Yeezy.fan, and contemporary press. Type markers are used throughout: **[Solo]**, **[Single]**, **[Feature]**, **[Remix]**, **[Mixtape]**, **[Leak/Unreleased]**, **[Listening Party]**, plus era tags ([WTT], [Cruel Summer], [TLOP], [ye], [KSG], [Donda], [Donda 2], [Vultures 1], [Vultures 2], [Cuck], [Bully]). Production-only credits where Kanye does not rap/sing are generally omitted. Where the original task brief mentioned tracks that turned out **not** to feature Kanye (e.g., Jay Electronica's *A Written Testimony*, Mary J. Blige's "Mr. Wrong," Drake's "Wants & Needs"), they are excluded with notes.

The 2025–26 era is presented under his preferred name **Ye**.

## Notes on items frequently misattributed to Kanye

A few items in the original brief are not actually Kanye features per verified sources: Jay Electronica's *A Written Testimony* (2020) features Jay-Z, not Kanye, on its uncredited verses; Mary J. Blige's "Mr. Wrong" (2011) features Drake, not Kanye; Drake's "Wants and Needs" (2021) only references Kanye lyrically; Nas's *King's Disease II* (2021) does not include Kanye on "Replace Me" or "Brunch on Sundays"; Big Sean's *Detroit 2* (2020) was Kanye-exec-produced but contains no Kanye verses on "ZTFO," "Lithuania," "Bigger Than Me," or "Sacrifices"; DJ Khaled's *Grateful* (2017) and *Khaled Khaled* (2021) contain no Kanye features; "Bye Bye (Remix)" by Mariah Carey has no Kanye verse; "Ghetto Dreams" (Common, 2011) features Nas; T.I.'s "Ball" / "Welcome to the Jungle" (2012) have no Kanye feature. The **"Use This Gospel" remix** with Eminem & Dr. Dre teased in 2020 was officially released on DJ Khaled's *God Did* in **August 2022**. **Mama's Boyfriend** is an MBDTF-era (2009–10) Q-Tip-produced song, not a 2007–08 track; multiple alternate versions (Madlib, Soulja Boy) exist and continue to surface — most recently a Mike Dean futuristic mix in April 2025.

This timeline reflects the most thoroughly documented body of Kanye West/Ye recordings and verses through May 7, 2026. Many further Vultures-era and Cuck-era alternate mixes exist on KTT2 and Yeezy.fan but lack verified lyrics or release status; for any deeper dive, those fan-curated resources remain the canonical reference.
